# Changelog

## 1.0.1

A small tidy-up release. Nothing new to learn, things just behave a bit better.

- Resizing the Dock is smoother. Clean Dock used to redo its settings preview on every step of the drag, even with the settings window closed.
- If your Dock uses magnification, the sharp icons now get out of the way as soon as you hover, instead of lingering over the enlarged ones for a moment.
- Clean Dock notices right away when the Dock restarts.
- The welcome window no longer switches "Launch at login" back on if you had turned it off.
- A big Downloads stack in the Dock costs less: the folder is only read again when something in it actually changed.
- Two folders with the same name now both show up properly under Permissions.
- The settings preview is always fresh when you open the window, also after switching between light and dark.
- Some housekeeping in the code.
